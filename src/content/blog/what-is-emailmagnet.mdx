EmailMagnet is a Chrome Extension that helps users find and extract emails from websites quickly while browsing. It is built for people who want to avoid manual copy-paste work when collecting visible email addresses from web pages.

## How EmailMagnet helps

- It detects emails from website content.
- It supports CSV and TXT export.
- The Free plan includes 200 emails per month.
- The PRO plan is currently positioned as $19 lifetime access.

## When to use it

EmailMagnet is useful when you are researching websites, preparing outreach lists, checking public contact pages, or collecting emails that are already visible on pages you visit.

## Responsible use

Users should follow applicable privacy and anti-spam laws, including GDPR and CAN-SPAM where relevant. EmailMagnet is a tool for extraction; responsible outreach practices remain the user's responsibility.
