Most email collection still happens the slow way: open a page, scan the text, copy an address, paste it somewhere else, repeat until the list is usable.

EmailMagnet exists to remove that repetitive middle layer.

> EmailMagnet is a Chrome extension for finding and extracting email addresses from websites while you browse.

It is built for people who work inside the browser: founders, sales teams, recruiters, researchers, operators, and anyone who regularly collects public contact information from web pages.

## The problem it solves

Email discovery is rarely difficult once you know where to look. The problem is that the process is fragmented.

You might find addresses on:

- company contact pages;
- directories;
- event pages;
- partner pages;
- profile pages;
- public team pages.

The manual workflow works for five contacts. It breaks down at fifty.

EmailMagnet turns that workflow into a browser action. Open the page, run extraction, review the results, and export.

## What EmailMagnet does

EmailMagnet detects email addresses from website content and brings them into a cleaner extraction workflow.

The core flow is simple:

1. Browse a website.
2. Open EmailMagnet.
3. Extract detected emails.
4. Export the results as CSV or TXT.

The goal is not to replace your CRM or outreach system. The goal is to make the collection step faster, cleaner, and less error-prone.

## Where it fits best

EmailMagnet is useful when you are already reviewing websites and need to capture contact data along the way.

Good-fit workflows include:

- building small prospecting lists;
- checking public company pages;
- researching partner or vendor contacts;
- collecting recruiting leads from public sources;
- preparing lightweight CSV files for follow-up.

It is intentionally browser-first. That matters because many teams do not need a heavy scraping stack. They need a faster way to handle the work they already do manually.

## Free and PRO in plain terms

The Free plan is built for trying the workflow and handling lighter extraction needs. It includes 200 emails per month and export up to 100 emails at once.

PRO is for recurring usage. It adds unlimited extraction, unlimited export size, autosave, bulk extraction, faster processing, and priority support.

The current PRO offer is a one-time $19 lifetime purchase.

## Responsible use still matters

EmailMagnet helps with extraction. It does not decide whether a contact should receive your message.

Before outreach, you should qualify the list, remove irrelevant contacts, respect opt-outs, and follow privacy and anti-spam rules such as GDPR and CAN-SPAM where applicable.

## The short version

EmailMagnet is for teams that want email extraction to feel like a normal part of browsing, not a separate research project.

Open the page. Extract the emails. Export the list. Move on.
