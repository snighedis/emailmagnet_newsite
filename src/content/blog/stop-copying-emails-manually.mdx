Manual email collection usually means opening a page, searching for contact details, copying addresses one by one, and pasting them into a file. EmailMagnet shortens that workflow inside Chrome.

## A simpler workflow

1. Install EmailMagnet.
2. Browse the websites you already need to review.
3. Extract emails from the page.
4. Export the results as CSV or TXT.

## Why it matters

The benefit is not a complex scraping setup. The benefit is reducing repetitive manual work during normal browsing sessions.
