The best plan is the one that matches the work you actually do.

Some users only need EmailMagnet occasionally. Others rely on email extraction every week as part of research, recruiting, prospecting, or operations.

> Upgrade when the limits start shaping your workflow instead of supporting it.

Here is a practical way to decide.

## Choose Free when extraction is occasional

The Free plan is built for light usage and evaluation.

It makes sense when:

- you extract emails a few times per month;
- your lists are small;
- exports rarely exceed 100 emails;
- you are still testing whether browser-based extraction fits your workflow.

Free gives you enough room to understand the product without committing to a paid plan.

## Choose PRO when extraction becomes part of the job

PRO is for recurring work.

It becomes the better fit when you need:

- unlimited extraction;
- unlimited export size;
- autosave while browsing;
- bulk extraction for larger URL sets;
- faster processing;
- priority email support.

The value is not just "more volume." It is fewer interruptions.

## The hidden cost of staying on the wrong plan

Free limits are useful when they protect a light workflow. They become expensive when they force workarounds.

Watch for these signals:

- you split exports into multiple files;
- you pause research because you hit a limit;
- you manually save results because autosave would be faster;
- you repeat the same extraction steps across many URLs;
- you spend more time managing the workflow than using the output.

When those patterns appear, the upgrade is no longer about features. It is about preserving momentum.

## A simple decision rule

Stay on Free if EmailMagnet is an occasional helper.

Upgrade to PRO if EmailMagnet is part of your weekly workflow.

The current PRO offer is a one-time $19 lifetime purchase, which makes the break-even point straightforward for anyone who extracts emails regularly.

## Bottom line

Free is for trying and light usage. PRO is for speed, scale, and repeatability.

Choose the plan that keeps your browser workflow moving.
