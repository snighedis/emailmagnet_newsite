# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Founders, solo operators, small sales/research teams, and ecommerce operators who work primarily in the browser and need practical tools that reduce repetitive manual work.

## Product Purpose
Dentoku Dev builds focused software products with clear scope and direct utility. The homepage and product pages should make each tool immediately understandable, show where it fits in daily workflows, and provide fast paths to try, evaluate, and get support.

## Brand Personality
Direct, practical, builder-minded. Confident without hype. Precise, calm, and useful.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS templates with neutral cards everywhere, hero metrics cliches, indistinct "AI startup" aesthetics, decorative gradient text, and copy-heavy sections that restate headlines.

## Design Principles
1. Show real workflow value early, avoid abstract claims.
2. Build strong visual hierarchy around actions and outcomes.
3. Keep sections structurally distinct so scroll feels intentional.
4. Preserve clarity first on mobile, then add desktop richness.
5. Keep every component tied to a specific user decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Avoid motion-dependent comprehension, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep touch targets readable and usable on small screens.
