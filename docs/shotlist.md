# Shot list: Dentoku Dev launch film, 25 s, 16:9

Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 750 frames. Grammar from `docs/style_guide.md`; content, copy,
logos and UI are Dentoku Dev's own. Music: original score per the brief in the style guide,
90 BPM, so **1 beat = 20 frames = 0.667 s** and every timecode below sits on that grid.

Palette: warm neutrals from the guide (`#1f1313`, `#514644`, `#ecebe6`, `#c1bfb0`) with
**Dentoku orange `#f05423` as the single accent** (caret, one word, the CTA pill). Product
colours appear only inside their own logos: Kallmy green `#0fb07b`, ShipVeryFast orange
`#f36414`.
Type: Inter 500, -0.03em, sentence case; Geist Mono for pill labels.

## Story

1. What Dentoku Dev makes, and that it is one studio.
2. What is already live: the four products on public stores (all on dentokudev.com today).
3. **Sneak peek** of what comes next: Kallmy and ShipVeryFast. They are not on dentokudev.com
   yet, so they are framed explicitly as a preview, never as part of the current portfolio.
4. More on the way, the studio's stance, the call to action.

On-screen copy is English; the Italian cut swaps the copy column only, same timings. Every
product claim comes from that product's own site.

## Sneak-peek rules

- Kallmy and ShipVeryFast always appear under a "SNEAK PEEK" / "ANTEPRIMA" pill (shots 5, 7, 9).
- They never appear in the live-portfolio shots (4 and 11) and are not counted among the
  products "on public stores".
- No launch date, price or availability claim is made for them on Dentoku's behalf; the only
  claims are what their own sites already state.
